# LittleGod game design — playable milestone

The guardian changes conditions rather than directing individual animals. A portrait isometric island floats against a quiet magical sky. Small expressive creatures, houses, flowers, rippling water, swaying foliage, and weather make consequences visible.

The core loop is observe → inspect → shape a habitat → watch a consequence → discover a species or settlement milestone → revisit the world. A reproducible seed and planet name give the world identity. There is no competitive loss condition or mandatory backend.

## Implemented rules

- Grassland: flowers, high plant growth, Mossbuns. Forest: dense trees and Lanternfoxes. Desert: dunes, cacti, low plant growth and Dunelings. Mountains: stone peaks and Pebblehorns. Snowlands: pale peaks, cold temperatures, slow growth and Frostpuffs. Wetlands: reeds, higher growth and Reedwings. The river is an additional water terrain.
- World ticks every two active seconds. Thirty ticks form a day; 120 ticks form a season. Rain restores moisture. Drought reduces moisture and vegetation. Seasonal growth rates alter resources. Vegetation feeds creatures; hunger affects migration and reproduction. Foxes raise local herbivore pressure. Populations have upper and lower bounds.
- Creatures wander and migrate to adjacent suitable habitats. Six initial species become discoverable over the first 75 ticks. Births add creatures if food is adequate. Deaths return individuals to the ecosystem while preserving a small resilient population.
- Communities gather renewable tile resources, consume food, grow, develop technology, share surplus with other settlements, and establish up to four communities. At 12 people a camp becomes a village; at 28 it becomes a town. Construction visibly adds houses. Blessing improves food and growth temporarily.
- Gifts: water (12 spirit), forest (15), mountain (18), wildlife (20), rain (22), civilization blessing (25). Spirit caps at 100 and regenerates 0.45 each tick. Gifts modify persistent simulation data, animate a ring and particles, and play synthesized audio.
- Discoveries and gifts award experience. Every 40 XP gains a level. Level two unlocks a violet sky cosmetic. Two initial milestones reward a new season and a sanctuary of 24 creatures. Aurora events appear every 150 ticks and render a sky effect.
- Offline time is capped at eight hours, advanced at one tick per real minute, with at most 480 ticks per return. Negative clock differences advance nothing. The return panel uses events generated by those ticks, never invented outcomes.

## Future content and monetization

Expand species diets and social behaviors, habitat transitions, hand-authored rare events, more achievements, and decorative customization. Cosmetic theme, building decoration, creature palette, and weather-skin catalogs can later share a local entitlement interface (`is_owned(id)`, `activate(id)`). Current unlocks use gameplay XP only. No purchase SDK, pricing, or real-money flow is implemented.

Retention should come from ecological stories and affection. No streak loss, energy sales, paid power advantages, or punitive absence mechanics.
