# Reforge usability pilot — planned, not conducted

Recruit five consenting participants unfamiliar with LittleGod after the full
guided loop exists. No participants have been recruited or messaged by this
work; no human feedback or usability success is claimed.

Give tasks without pointing out controls: find Arin, understand the current
objective, navigate to it, perform it, claim a legitimate reward, find an unlocked
hunt, select/fight/dodge, pick up loot, return, inspect/equip an improvement,
locate the smith and find the next useful kingdom action.

Record per task: success/partial/failure, time to first correct action, wrong
routes, help requests, unreadable text, missed taps, subjective combat response,
audio comfort and the participant's explanation of the next objective.

Feedback form:

* Device / viewport / language / accessibility preferences:
* Task and time started / ended:
* What did you expect the next tap to do?
* What happened instead?
* Which text or icon was unclear?
* Did you understand the goal and reward?
* Was movement/combat comfortable?
* Were sounds comfortable at your chosen volume?
* Would you know what to do after returning?

Bug template: build/checkpoint, device/OS, exact steps, expected/actual behavior,
frequency, save context (without personal secrets), screenshot/video, severity.
Automation validates mechanics and layouts; it does not establish enjoyment or
first-time comprehension.
