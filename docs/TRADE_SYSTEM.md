# E1 trade, caravans and inventory recovery

After the twenty ordered claims, explicit Chapter V opt-in adds `kingdom.trade.version=1`. Existing kingdom version3, stock, hero, company, item IDs and forge queues remain unchanged. Realm cap is21 for this slice. The authentic D fixture was generated at `38c7f0d` before trade code existed; the fixture generator refuses to run against newer code.

The Lantern Marketplace costs16 wood/8 stone/3 gold, uses real construction labor, and has two merchant slots. A merchant is an ordinary canonical citizen. Local transactions need a merchant actually present at the completed market. Six ordinary goods are tradable: wood, stone, ore, iron, leather and herbs. Unique heart/quest stock and artifact equipment are excluded. Buy/sell quantities must be whole positive1–20 units; balances and storage are checked before mutation. Prices use existing gold in thousandths. Every route export price is below the local buy price; a retained test checks all routes/goods against buy-and-resell arbitrage.

Two data-defined strategic routes connect the River Guild and Iron Hills trading post. The trading post is not a new playable region. Capacity is eight units, fee1 gold. River takes12 ticks each way, Hills18, on the existing two-second normal-speed clock. Supplies are prepaid0.1 food per travel tick (2.4/3.6 food round trip). Dispatch validates all costs/ownership, then atomically reserves cargo, fee, food, merchant and up to two ready soldiers. At most two caravans are pending; twelve bounded records preserve recent history. Unique counters never reuse IDs.

State flow is `outbound → returning → ready → collected`; weaker escorts instead reach `blocked`. Health-scaled actual army attack/armor determines deterrence against route danger18/35. This is a deterministic road-safety decision, not an imaginary battle or random casualties. At a blocked road the player may pay1.2 food for a detour adding six ticks to each leg, or return the unsold actual cargo. No unattended combat, injury or destructive loss happens offline. Loading does not reroll the encounter. Guard assignment cannot be duplicated across routes, deployed into battle, dismissed or healed while away. A traveling merchant cannot produce or switch jobs.

Arrival sells the reserved cargo into a locked quote; the return brings the payment into escrow. Only collecting at home credits gold and increments delivered. Repeated collection/quest claim is rejected. Retreat returns cargo instead of a sale payment and refunds unused prepaid food, capped at food storage. It waits safely if returning cargo cannot fit. Completed return releases merchant/guards. Tick updates are idempotent; validation checks shapes, route IDs, unique reservations, cargo/quotes, states, progress and supply arithmetic.

Reserved merchant jobs still occupy the market's two physical slots; they cannot create a third assignment that invalidates a save. Pending reservations also block market upgrades until collection. An already-returned merchant may buy/sell locally while their cargo awaits collection, so full storage can be cleared even if both merchants were away. They remain unavailable for another caravan or job until collection. These capacity/upgrade/warehouse edge cases have dedicated assertions.

Menus normally pause work. Live Route Watch explicitly advances the same home simulation and draws a bounded strategic wagon animation from real progress. It temporarily hides/suspends the covered planet renderer; closing it restores rendering and the prior pause flag. Wildwood/army battles retain their original home/offline pause policy, including caravan travel. Ordinary offline progress can finish a safe caravan into escrow; blocked routes await a decision.

Unused-item salvage is available at a merchant or staffed forge, including before Chapter V. It removes exactly one unreserved/unequipped item and refunds up to half its ordinary crafting inputs, limited by storage. The confirmation states that overflow is discarded. Artifacts, hero gear, citizen tools and army kits remain protected; pending jobs retain their IDs/progress. This frees a full32-slot inventory without requiring completion of D/E first. No cancellation/replay creates items or currency.

Implementation: `scripts/trade.gd`, `trade_interface.gd`, `caravan_view.gd`; route data in `assets/kingdom/trade_routes.json`. New original SVGs: marketplace and wagon/merchant. Existing art/fonts/audio are retained. Save fixtures and tests cover migration, escrow, absence safety, risk decisions, reservations, full inventory, artifacts and exactly-once delivery. Run `devtools/verify_trade.py` for retained and new suites.
