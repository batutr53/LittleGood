# Character style study — V4B, unapproved

A compact teal-cloaked, slate-armored Warrior with pale metal facets and warm brass accents. This follows the existing island's teal/sage/cream palette; it is a style study, not an approved V3 replacement. The existing generated Warrior PNG remains available and is the default.

Original source: `devtools/create_v4_warrior.py`. Six atlas SVGs and `assets/visual_v4/warrior-poses.json` describe 144 reproducible poses. Four cardinal facing treatments are supported; no eight-direction production animation is promised. The intentionally simplified small-scale face and armor avoid unstable details. Body sections and limb endpoints are authored together; the character is not a rotating full-body PNG.

Limitations: side views share much of the front silhouette; no unique armor meshes or exact weapon silhouettes per recipe. All currently implemented warrior weapons use a generic attached sword silhouette, with restrained recipe tints; all shields share a generic shield shape. Missing equipment hides its corresponding visual. The armor is a fixed style-study shell. These fallbacks are explicit, and equipment ownership remains authoritative.

Rendered review found readable alternating feet and stable proportions, but the flat vector style needs an art approval and further craft before a premium production claim. Kneeling defeat is a presentation test pose, not the final collapse animation.
