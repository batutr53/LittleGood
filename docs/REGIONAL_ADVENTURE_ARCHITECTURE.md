# Regional adventures — E2B checkpoint

This checkpoint implements E2A and the playable Iron Hills slice E2B. It does not implement Ashen Desert or quest22. Campaign progression remains1–21. The complete received scope is in MILESTONE_E2_BRIEF.md.

`scripts/regions.gd` is a trusted local registry. Stable IDs are `wildwood`, `iron_hills`, `ashen_desert`; the last is explicitly unavailable. Data supplies localized names, previews, recommendations, resources, enemies and landmarks. There is no downloaded script execution. Iron Hills entry requires all21 ordered claims and the existing armed Warrior. Entry has no additional travel fee. The world map separates hero destinations from the unchanged E1 trading posts and links to the original caravan UI.

`adventure.gd` retains the original20Hz hero/combat/loot model. `adventure_scene.gd` retains CharacterBody2D movement, native joystick, attack, dodge, healing, camera and HUD. The selected map supplies static bodies and the headless slide/path helpers. The mountain scene has bounded960×1440 geometry, narrow passages, ore-bearing rocks, pines, abandoned rails/cart and a torch-lit mine. Live actors are sorted against props; artwork is not a substitute for collision or navigation. Original vector terrain is imported once instead of submitting hundreds of static circle commands every frame.

E1 records are not automatically rewritten. The first actual regional switch adds optional `kingdom.adventure.regions = {version:1, stored:{...}}`. The active/current expedition stays solely in `adventure.run`; an inactive expedition is moved into `stored` and removed when selected again. At most two stored records exist; active or duplicate region records are rejected. Legacy runs without a region field mean Wildwood. Existing boss history, unique equipment, crafting jobs, military and merchant assignments, escorts, cargo reservations, cosmetics and world records retain their ownership.

Iron Hills adds `region` and two boolean `nodes` to the ordinary run. It reuses ordinary `ore` and `stone`, existing cargo and stock. Gathering requires an alive hero within85 world units. Capacity uses the kingdom's actual per-resource warehouse limit including cargo already reserved in the expedition. No node is depleted unless its capacity-checked pickup succeeds. The two finite deposits yield6 ore and5 stone total. They **never refresh in E2B**, including leaving, offline processing, loading or the Wildwood refresh command. Enemy rewards also remain tied to the persisted run. New repeatable regional production is outside this checkpoint; no refresh clock is fabricated.

Resources enter existing cargo and are banked once by the original home-return operation. Ore feeds the existing furnace, then forge; stone pays existing construction costs. No second inventory, new currency or unused exotic material is introduced. The Forgotten Mine is an actual reachable interaction; its persistent landmark flag and bounded kingdom chronicle entry record discovery once.

Clock policy remains unchanged: an active adventure (any selected region), including a defeated hero awaiting return, pauses home ticks, military/industry production, offline progress and caravan travel. Explicit adventure pause also stops the regional model. Home return restores the saved home pause preference and health, banks cargo and leaves the region record intact. E1 Live Route Watch separately advances home/travel ticks while watching, then restores the prior pause state. These two policies must stay separate when E2C is implemented.

Tests: `tests/regions.gd`, `tests/regional_combat.gd`, `tests/regions_playtest.gd`. Android: `scripts/regions_qa.gd` and `devtools/android_regions_validate.py`; only the isolated `.betaqa` package may be cleared. Bot navigation and external ADB touchscreen inputs are labeled separately. A real force-stop/relaunch checks active expedition restoration before a native Home tap.
