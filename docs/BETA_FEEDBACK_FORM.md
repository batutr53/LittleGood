# LittleGod 0.6 — voluntary feedback

No name, email, account ID or private save is required. Share this form and optional diagnostics only if you choose. Do not publish another person's planet or names without their consent.

- Build/version, optional device model and OS:
- Were you new to this kind of game?
- What did you try in the first two minutes? Where did you hesitate?
- What changed after your first gift? Could you explain why?
- Which creature or community do you remember? What made it memorable?
- Did favorite, biography, Chronicle and Wonders help you find something to care about?
- What did you discover without being told a trigger?
- Could you export a planet, import the example and return to your own planet? Was read-only visiting clear?
- Did you understand that reactions stay on your device?
- Could you save and manually share a postcard? Were public names clear?
- Touch comfort, visual clarity and enjoyment: rate each 1–5 and explain.
- Where did the game hitch, misread a gesture or lose state? Exact reproduction steps:
- What made you stop? What would you choose to do next?
- If you returned voluntarily later, what prompted it and what changed? No return is also useful feedback.
- Any other criticism or accessibility needs:

Optional attachments: reviewed screenshot; manually reviewed local diagnostic export. Remove personal text first. A return-day marker describes one installation and is not a cohort retention statistic. An unclean-session marker does not prove a crash.
